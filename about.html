Our Founding Story

Built on Expertise. Driven by Impact.

Ruach Business Consortia Limited was established with one clear purpose: to solve the persistent gap between strategic intent and operational execution in African enterprises and institutions. Founded by seasoned management executives with decades of cross-sector experience spanning agribusiness, energy, finance, healthcare, and international development, Ruach was built as a consortium of expertise — not just a consultancy.

With a global advisory network spanning Nigeria, Dubai, London, and Canada — we work at the intersection of strategy, digital transformation, and human capital to deliver outcomes that outlast any single engagement.

Registered under the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria (RC: 1488920), we operate across three core service pillars: Business Consulting & eBAS, Executive Training & Masterclasses, and Enterprise Digital Transformation.

Why Choose Ruach

What Sets Ruach Apart

In a market crowded with generalist consultancies, Ruach is built differently — combining deep sector expertise, proprietary digital tools, and a structured capacity building methodology.

Consortium Model — Not a Solo Firm
Ruach operates as a consortium of specialists, bringing together sector-specific experts for every engagement — meaning our clients benefit from deep, multi-disciplinary capability rather than a single generalist perspective.
Proprietary eBAS Digital Platform
We don't just advise — we deploy our own Enterprise Business Analytics Solutions (eBAS) platform, giving clients a live digital management command center that makes our consulting recommendations measurable and self-sustaining.
Integrated Training & Consulting Ecosystem
Our executive training and business consulting arms operate in tandem — meaning client organizations benefit from both strategic advisory at the institutional level and leadership capacity building at the individual level simultaneously.
African Context, Global Standards
We combine deep Nigerian and African market intelligence with internationally certified methodologies and a global advisory network spanning Dubai, London, and Canada — delivering world-class solutions that are locally grounded.
Results-Oriented Delivery Model
Every Ruach engagement begins with a baseline diagnostic, deploys data-driven interventions, and closes with a measurable KPI dashboard — ensuring accountability for outcomes, not just deliverables.
Long-Term Partnership Approach
With a 98% client retention rate, Ruach builds multi-year relationships with clients — acting as a trusted institutional partner across strategy, execution, training, and digital governance over the long term.
